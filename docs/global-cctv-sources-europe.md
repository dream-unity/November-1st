# European public live-camera provenance — 17 September 2026

The reviewed candidate set contains **38 original public video players across 22 European countries**. Each candidate's public YouTube watch-page response returned `playabilityStatus.status = OK`, `playableInEmbed = true`, `videoDetails.isLive = true`, and `microformat.playerMicroformatRenderer.liveBroadcastDetails.isLiveNow = true` during checks on 17 September 2026. The broadcaster supplies the original player; no media URLs, authentication tokens or protected streams were extracted.

These checks establish **provider-declared current live broadcasting and permission to use the original embedded player**. They are **not decoded-frame or movement verification for every camera**, a blanket redistribution licence, a guarantee of uninterrupted availability, or proof that all broadcaster content is unrepeated. The release's separate browser verification record describes actual player tests. Country coverage means one or more listed public cameras in that country, not nationwide street CCTV coverage.

## Errors and rejected candidates

- `W_TvWaw_CC0` advertises Tallinn live video in its title, but its actual `isLiveContent` was false; excluded.
- `j9JiEJ7vbFQ` was catalogued as a children's ski slope in Čierny Balog, but the current original video is titled `PADEL` under Jahodna Ski. Excluded because its published identity no longer matches the candidate.
- A Madeira Airport camera resolved to scheduled broadcast `-AoVEG70Tws`, reporting `LIVE_STREAM_OFFLINE` and an upcoming start. It was not counted as current continuous live footage.
- Several discovery-directory records had incorrect geography: Baltimore, Maryland was assigned to West Cork, Ireland; the Lahinch kestrel camera was assigned to Palmerstown; and Oudeschild harbour was assigned to De Waal. The first was excluded; the latter two were corrected from broadcaster information. Discovery directory data is not treated as an authoritative camera-location registry.
- Public image-only feeds, finite video loops, protected Skyline streams, and undefined camera ownership were excluded. Skyline's third-party still-image offering does not establish live-video embedding permission.
- Live From Iceland publishes original IPCamLive players for Austurvöllur and Langihryggur, but they are not included in this YouTube-focused release. No attempt was made to bypass their player or extract protected media.

## Integration requirements and limits

- Use the original HTTPS YouTube player, with normal player controls and branding. Supply a genuine browser referrer using `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`; `no-referrer` can trigger YouTube Error 153. A server request without a referrer is not representative of an embedded browser player.
- Camera coordinates are approximate viewing locations, rounded to three decimal places. Some identify a locality rather than the camera hardware. Wildlife markers indicate only the general viewing area; they are not precise nest locations.
- Sports and wildlife cameras are named as such. They supplement the public city, transport, beach, harbour and landscape cameras; they are not claimed to be street-surveillance cameras.
- A provider may restart a broadcast with a new video ID, take a camera offline, disable embedding, require consent or restrict playback geographically. A failed player must show an honest failure with its original-source link, never silently become a snapshot or a clip.
