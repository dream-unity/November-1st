A quick introduction

Welcome to God’s Earth View

Explore Earth on an interactive globe. Follow aircraft and satellites, listen to radio from other places, and watch public cameras. You choose what to explore, at your own pace.

  1. Start with a place

    Open LOCATION, search for a city or landmark, and choose a result. Drag the globe to look around. Scroll to zoom, or use one finger to move and two fingers to pinch on a touch screen.

  2. Add something to explore

    Open DATA LAYERS with its + button. Try one layer first, such as aircraft, satellites or earthquakes. Select a marker for more information. When following an aircraft, Cockpit offers another view.

  3. Listen, watch and check the roads

    Use Live radio, CCTV cameras or Traffic reports near the top of the screen. Search or filter by place, then choose a station, camera or report. Cameras start with the Live video filter; snapshots are listed separately.

  4. Keep it simple

    You do not need every panel at once. Close a panel when you have finished. Use the globe button at the top to return to a view of Earth. Explore VISUAL PRESETS or SCENES when you feel ready.

The information comes from public sources. Coverage and update times vary; not every view is live. Some optional features, including AI voice, may be unavailable. Data sources explains what is connected.
